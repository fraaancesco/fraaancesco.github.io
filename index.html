Nice to meet you.

I'm Francesco, a Computer Engineer from Catania. For the past few years I've been a software engineer at Fincons Group, mostly on the backend: building REST APIs in Java and TypeScript, looking after PostgreSQL and MongoDB, and jumping into Vue.js when the frontend needs a hand.

I like understanding how things work — and how they break. It's a curiosity I've had since I was a kid, and now I'm turning it into real study: I'm learning cybersecurity on TryHackMe and, whenever I can, putting it into practice by writing small open-source tools in Go.

Away from the keyboard: painting, mountain trails, and thriller or horror films — the darker, the better.
