01 About

Curious by design.
Backend by trade.

I'm a Computer Engineer from Catania who has spent the last years at Fincons Group building the parts of software most people never see — REST APIs, services and databases — while still shipping the interfaces on top of them.

My fascination with security and technology started in childhood, and it never really left. Today it drives what I do after hours: practising offensive security on TryHackMe and writing security tooling in Go.

I care about communication, teamwork, autonomy and solving problems properly — and I'm happiest when a new challenge forces me to learn something I didn't know yesterday.
